### Words of affirmation

<div align="center">"The object of love is not to get something you want, but doing something for the well-being of the one you love."</div>

1. **Verbal compliments** (appreciation)\
   Every time someone you love does something good, tell them you appreciate them
    
3. **Words of encouragement**\
   Try to see the world from the other person's perspective.
   Learn what is important to the other person.
   "I know. I care. I am with you. How can I help?"

3. **Kind words**\
   Be attentive to the tone.
   Share hurt, pain, and anger using honest, kind words (e.g., "I felt disappointed and hurt that...").
   When partner is angry and upset, use a **soft** voice as it turns away anger.
   Then try to put yourself in your partner's perspective of the events to understand **why** the other person feels that way.
   **When wronged**: You can choose justice or forgiveness when wronged. Forgiveness is the way of love.
   **When you do something wrong**: confess, ask for forgiveness, and try to act differently in future.
   Don't pollute today with yesterday's failures.
   You have to know the partner's desires and wants.
   
> [!NOTE]
> “I love you. I care about you, and I choose to forgive you.
> Even though my feelings of hurt may linger, I will not allow what has happened to come between us.
> I hope that we can learn from this experience.
> You are not a failure because you have failed.
> You are my spouse, and together we will go on from here.” 
   
4. **Humble words**
   Know each other's desires and wants.
   Make wants, needs, and desires known by making requests to give guidance, not demands or ultimatums.
   Make requests by affirming the other person's worth or abilities.

5. **Various dialects**

<div align="center">"Possibly the deepest human need is the need to feel appreciated"</div>

   Write down words of affirmation in a notebook.
   Say positive things about the person
   (1) directly to the person,
   (2) indirectly to others when they are not present,
   (3) to others when they are present (when receiving, share the credit)

   Create a list of positive traits and add to them.
